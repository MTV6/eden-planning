# 📜 EDEN — RAID LEADER DEDUCTION RULES
Short rules first. Numbered explanations follow. RL = raid leader; LB = lootbody.

## ⚔️ Reasons and shares
**1. Avoidable death — 0.5 share.**
The player must be responsible for an avoidable death without major wipe impact.

**2. Mistake causing or contributing to a wipe — 1 share.**
Establish the player's contribution. It does not have to be the sole cause.

**3. Failed mechanic or assignment — apply the impact scale.**
Includes soaks, interrupts, dispels, taunts, positioning, target swaps and assigned utility.
• Avoidable death without major wipe impact: **0.5 share**.
• Ignoring a mechanic and killing someone on a kill pull: **0.5 share**.
• Causing or contributing to a wipe: **1 share**.
• An isolated failure without a death or wipe is not automatically deductible. Repeated disregard of an understood assignment can warrant **1 share**.

**4. Unused/mistimed defensive, potion or Healthstone — same 0.5 / 1 scale.**
Only when proper use could reasonably have prevented the outcome. This supports the incident finding; it is **not an additional charge for the same incident**.

**5. Voice violation — 1 share for the run.**
Separate qualifying mechanic mistakes can receive additional deductions.

**6. Preparation violation — 1 share for the run.**
Meet agreed character, role, spec, gear and consumable requirements. Do not charge again for every boss.

**7. Lateness — 1 share for each boss missed.**
The RL may be lenient depending on the reason.

**8. Conduct violation — normally 1 share across the run.**
Separate, serious, unrelated offenses may warrant further deductions.

**9. DC causing a preventable wipe — 1 share.**
The disconnect must cause a missed mechanic and a wipe that continued play could reasonably have prevented. A DC alone is not enough.

## ✅ When deductions apply
**10. Wipe-pull trigger: 3 wipes on one boss OR 6 across the run.**
The RL confirms deliberate resets and aborted pulls in the final review sheet. **Confirmed resets/aborts are excluded from the wipe count.**

**11. Earlier pulls and kill pulls count.**
Once the wipe trigger is reached, review earlier qualifying wipe-pull mistakes **across the entire run**. Kill-pull mistakes are independently eligible, even before the threshold or in a zero-wipe run. Review everyone, including the RL.

**12. Pre-trigger exceptions.**
Damage shortfall causing a **1% wipe**, or insufficient healing causing **multiple avoidable deaths**, can qualify before the wipe threshold.
Voice, preparation, attendance and conduct violations can also apply before it. **Try warnings first; the RL may exercise leniency.**

**13. Stop assessment once the pull is unrecoverable or a wipe is called.**
Later deaths are not additional performance mistakes merely because players continued dying.

**14. Separate incidents add together; no multipliers.**
Do not charge one incident repeatedly as a mechanic failure, death and wipe.

**15. Total first, then round the final deduction DOWN.**
• 0.5 + 0.5 = **1 share removed**.
• 1.5 becomes **1**; 2.5 becomes **2**.
• A lone 0.5 becomes **0 whole shares removed**.
Keep the exact incident total in the record. Never invent another mistake to reach a whole share.

**16. Establish responsibility.**
Being killed by another player's mistake or a wrong RL call does not automatically make the victim responsible. Shared responsibility requires a separate, evidenced contribution from each person.

## ⚔️ Performance and roster
**17. Meet the agreed requirements for the run.**
Management sets bot requirements and roster ratios. RLs must not manipulate them to increase their own cut.

**18. Judge useful performance, not just the overall parse.**
Consider required boss/add damage, assignments, encounter importance and actual damage/healing needs. A high parse does not excuse neglecting mechanics or important targets. There is no universal flat deduction merely for a low parse.

**19. Heroic VIP baseline: 13 boosters / 3 LBs.**
With four buyers, this makes 20 players. The RL must ensure adequate healing, damage, utility and mechanic coverage, and account for saved players affecting loot eligibility.

**20. RL mistakes are reviewable and deductible too.**
Apply the same responsibility and impact principles to calls, assignments, markers, positioning and roster decisions.

## 🔄 Replacements
**21. Decide the outgoing player's cut based on severity and circumstances.**
Record their final entitlement separately from the replacement's payment.

**22. An unsaved replacement needed for loot eligibility can justify full-cut removal.**
This is an RL decision, not an automatic result of every replacement.

**23. Agree replacement pay in advance and post it in the run channel.**
• **Unsaved replacement:** full cut unless they explicitly volunteer to take less.
• **Saved replacement:** full cut or a minimum of **3/8 of a normal cut**, unless they voluntarily agree to less or to help free.

**24. Removal without a replacement can leave half cut.**
If the run finishes without replacing the player, the RL may allow half cut depending on severity. A severe offense can result in full-cut removal.

---

## 🔄 Replacement totals and staff help
**25. Half cut = half retained; full-cut removal = zero retained.**
For an ordinary eight-share entitlement: **4 retained** or **0 retained**.
Half-cut removal can be followed by further deductions for separate rule violations. **Never reduce the final cut below zero.**
After full-cut removal, record other mistakes and whether they contributed to the removal; do not deduct further shares.

**26. Contact staff when a run takes too long or a boss exceeds five wipes.**
A staff ping with **“Need help with people”** or another brief request is enough. No detailed report is required. Staff not responding is not the RL's fault after a reasonable attempt.
If the roster makes a run unviable and cancellation is necessary, cancel **at least one hour before start**.

## 📹 Evidence and appeals
**27. Log the run and provide a recording with Discord voice.**
The recording may be yours or another player's. Retain it for **48 hours after deductions are posted**, and preserve relevant evidence longer during an active dispute.
Staff can decide using Warcraft Logs if recording fails; recordings add evidence to the case.

**28. Document every deduction.**
Record the player, run, boss/pull, timestamp, mistake, impact, evidence and shares removed/remaining. Include assignments where relevant and preserve exact totals before rounding.

**29. Staff reviews appeals first, then brings in the RL to answer.**
The **staff council/Sunking have the final word**.

**30. Correct unprocessed attendance; otherwise, the RL refunds personally.**
Contact Sunking promptly to correct shares before attendance is processed. If attendance has been sent and cannot be reversed, the RL refunds the affected player **within 24 hours of the refund decision**. Do not take the money from other players' balances.

**31. Corrections replace previous amounts.**
Never add a revised deduction to its original amount. Preserve the correction history.

---

## 💰 Deducted gold
**32. Undeducted RL: 70% to non-deducted boosters; 30% to the RL.**
The RL receives only that **30%**, with no additional portion of the boosters' pool.

**33. Deducted RL: 100% to non-deducted boosters.**
The RL receives **no redistribution**.

**34. Deducted players receive no redistributed deduction gold.**
This includes proceeds from their own deduction and other players' deductions. If **everyone is deducted, the gold goes to Eden management**.
These rules concern extra deducted gold, not the ordinary cut a player retains.

## 📋 Assignments, attendance and appeals
**35. Make assignments clear before the pull.**
Discord/in-game text, images, NSRT notes or clear voice assignments are acceptable. Keep a screenshot or other record where possible.

**36. Handle lateness and repeated no-shows consistently.**
• While clients are still being invited, the RL may wait up to **15 minutes after start time**.
• If everyone else is ready, after **5 minutes** the RL may replace the missing player or start without them.
• Apply rule 7 to bosses missed, with leniency where justified.
• **2 no-shows in the preceding 2 weeks → 1-week access suspension.**
• **4+ no-shows in the preceding month → 1-month access suspension.**
Use **rolling windows**. The monthly suspension **replaces**, rather than stacks with, the weekly suspension.

**37. Appeals: 48 hours from the deduction-channel post.**
The clock starts when the player's deduction is posted in the deduction channel, not when the run ends.

**38. Establish who each participant is.**
Review boosters, helpers and RLs. Buyers and LBs are excluded from deduction candidates. Unmatched characters remain **unknown** until classified. Do not guess that an unrostered player is a buyer or link an alt without confirmation.

## 📖 Explanations — responsibility and mechanics
**Rules 1–4 and 16**
> Work backwards from the outcome to the underlying mistake. Check the assignment, positioning, incoming damage, healing and available responses. A death alone does not prove fault.

> If another player creates the danger, do not automatically blame the victim. However, a player who had a reasonable opportunity to respond to a known danger may also be responsible for a separate failure to adapt. Review this case by case.

> If three players' separate mistakes collectively cause a wipe, each may receive **1 share**, provided each contribution is established. This does not mean every player who died receives a deduction.

> A first acknowledged mechanic failure with no death or wipe may be forgiven. Repeatedly disregarding an understood assignment can become an instruction/preparation issue worth **1 share**. Do not also charge the same failure again under another label.

> If a player ignores a mechanic and kills someone but the boss still dies, the responsible player may receive **0.5 share**. The victim is not automatically deducted.

**Rule 4 — defensives and self-healing**
> For foreseeable dangerous damage, consider whether a defensive, potion or Healthstone could reasonably have prevented the death, including availability and the opportunity to react.

> If the hit would kill through the available defensive, investigate why the player was hit. The absence of a defensive cast does not by itself prove that the ability was ready or would have saved them.

> Defensive non-use is evidence supporting the original **0.5 / 1-share incident**, not an extra fine.

---

## 📖 Explanations — voice, preparation and conduct
**Rules 5–8 and 12**
> Communicate expectations and try warnings before strict enforcement. Voice, preparation, attendance and conduct rules can apply before the wipe threshold, but the RL may be lenient.

> A player who knows the fight, causes no problems and was never specifically asked to join voice should not automatically be punished simply because their absence is discovered afterwards. Agreed exceptions are possible. Ask them to join and follow up when necessary.

> An applicable voice violation is **1 share for the run**. A separate qualifying mechanic mistake can add its own deduction. Do not charge another voice share for every wipe.

> For missing preparation, start with a polite warning where appropriate, especially when an otherwise reliable player can correct forgotten consumables or preparation. The preparation deduction is **1 share for the run**, not per boss.

> Lateness is **1 share per boss missed**, with RL leniency depending on the explanation. Keep this distinct from a replacement/full-cut decision so the final entitlement is clear.

> Explain mistakes calmly and specifically. Repeated problems may justify warning that replacement will follow. Criticize the action, not the person; personal insults, abuse, harassment and sustained targeting are unacceptable.

> Conduct normally receives **1 share across the run**. Separate, serious and unrelated offenses can warrant further deductions. Explain each such offense rather than counting repeated remarks about the same incident as automatic extra charges.

---

## 📖 Explanations — wipes and totals
**Rules 10–13**
> The RL confirms deliberate resets and aborted pulls in the final review sheet; confirmed resets/aborts do not count towards the **3-on-one-boss / 6-across-the-run** trigger.

> Once either trigger is reached, earlier qualifying wipe-pull mistakes across the **entire run** become reviewable. Kill-pull mistakes remain eligible independently. Review everyone fairly, including the RL.

> Judge recoverability using surviving tanks/healers/DPS, combat resurrections, required soaks or other mechanics, damage/healing checks, remaining boss health, enrage and difficulty.

> One or two deaths do not automatically make a Heroic pull unrecoverable. Conversely, a death that leaves the raid unable to perform a required mechanic may do so. Multiple staff members can review disputed cases.

> Once the pull is unrecoverable or a wipe is called, subsequent deaths are not new performance offenses merely because players continued dying.

**Rules 14–15**
> Add separate qualifying incidents with **no multipliers**. One failed mechanic leading to a death and a wipe is one causal incident, not three charges. A separate voice violation can still be recorded alongside it.

> Add exact half shares before rounding the final deduction **down**. For an eight-share entitlement and **2.5** in incident deductions: remove **2**, retain **6**, and preserve the **2.5** evidence total.

> Do not round each incident individually and do not search for an excuse to raise a fractional total. Another deduction needs another genuine, independently supported violation.

---

## 📖 Explanations — performance and leading
**Rules 17–20 and 35**
> Judge performance against the agreed needs of the run, not only the overall parse. Necessary add damage is useful damage. Damage on irrelevant targets to improve a parse is different.

> Consider encounter importance and the actual damage/healing check. A high parse does not excuse holding cooldowns for irrelevant adds, neglecting important boss damage or ignoring assignments. Low boss damage alone does not prove padding when add damage was required.

> Management sets roster requirements and ratios. The RL must assess whether the actual roster can complete the run, including healing, utility, mechanic coverage and loot eligibility. Saved status must be disclosed and agreed.

> **13 boosters / 3 LBs is the Heroic VIP baseline.** Do not treat it as a universal roster format.

> Faulty calls, unclear assignments and unsuitable roster decisions matter when assigning responsibility. RL mistakes are reviewed under the same impact principles as other players' mistakes.

> Assignments may be given in Discord/in-game text, NSRT notes or voice, provided they are clear and understood before the pull. A screenshot of a WoWUtils layout or other assignment record helps resolve disputes.

**Rules 26 and 36**
> Waiting while clients are still being invited is different from delaying an otherwise ready raid. Use the stated time limits with reasonable judgement.

> A simple staff ping and request for help is sufficient. You may also contact available staff directly. You are not responsible for them failing to respond after a reasonable attempt.

---

## 📖 Explanations — replacements and refunds
**Rules 21–25**
> The outgoing player's entitlement and the incoming replacement's payment are separate decisions. Agree payment before the replacement joins and post it in the run channel.

> An unsaved replacement receives a full cut unless they explicitly volunteer for less. A saved replacement can receive full cut or at least **3/8**, unless they voluntarily agree otherwise. Do not assume someone will accept reduced or unpaid help.

> A half-cut removal can be followed by further deductions for separate, evidenced rule violations. Once the retained cut reaches **zero**, no further shares can be deducted.

> After full-cut removal, still record additional mistakes and explain which contributed to the removal. Do not present the full-cut adjustment as proof of an equivalent number of one-share mechanic offenses.

**Rules 27–31 and 37**
> Warcraft Logs can be used by staff to decide the case even if recording failed. Video adds context, especially for calls and assignments. Base the decision on the available evidence.

> Keep recordings for **48 hours after deductions are posted**. During an active dispute, preserve relevant video longer and ask other participants for their recordings if useful.

> Staff reviews the appeal first, then asks the RL to explain. Staff/Sunking make the final decision. Disagreement or good faith does not remove the refund obligation after an overturned deduction.

> Correct shares before attendance is processed whenever possible. If attendance has been sent and cannot be reversed, the RL pays the difference personally **within 24 hours of the refund decision**. Do not reduce other players' balances to fund it.

> An RL who disagrees may discuss the reasoning with staff and request a rule clarification. A revised decision replaces the old amount; it is never added on top.
